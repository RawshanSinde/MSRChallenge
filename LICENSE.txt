The individual spec files, commit messages, and other repository content
included or referenced in this dataset remain the property of their respective
authors and are subject to the license of each source repository. Each row
retains its repository's SPDX license identifier (repo_license_spdx_id) so that
users can filter to license-compatible subsets. Redistribution or reuse of any

GitHub's Terms of Service, not by the CC BY 4.0 license that covers the

All data was collected from public GitHub repositories within the GitHub API
terms of service. Commit email addresses, avatar URLs, and GPG signatures have
been removed; public author and committer logins and numeric identifiers are
retained. Users must not attempt to deanonymize individuals and must keep
personal data out of published results beyond aggregate analysis.
